About
I trained to price risk before I learned to model it. That order is probably why you should hire me.
Explainable AI Risk & InsurTech Human Behaviour Policy Analytics

I'm an actuary who learned to code, not a coder who picked up statistics. An actuary is trained to ask "what happens if I'm wrong, and who pays for it?" before anything else. I spent three years asking only that question, before I ever touched a model.

Today, I build production software at iLabAfrica, Strathmore University, end to end, database through dashboard, for clients across government, healthcare, and conservation, and decide where the machine learning sits inside it. I'm also part of Vondetta, an AI research brand where a small group of Kenyan engineers builds homegrown, grassroots decision-support AI, for everyone from a mama mboga tracking her margins to a county official setting policy.

My rule: if a client can't follow why my system made a decision, I haven't finished the job.

The Journey

The problems I was actually handed, roughly in the order they happened.

Jun 2022 – Jul 2024
Claims Analyst · Kenyatta National Hospital
A national hospital processes a huge volume of insurance claims, some honest, some not. I built the pipelines that cleaned the data and flagged unusual claims before they were paid out, not after.
This is where I learned a model only matters if it catches the problem in time.
Aug – Dec 2024
Junior Underwriter · Incourage Insurance
Someone has to decide whether to accept an application, and at what price. I applied statistical risk assessment to real applications, learning the judgment calls an underwriter has to defend.
This is where "explainable AI" stopped being a buzzword. I'd sat in the underwriter's chair myself.
Dec 2024 – May 2025
Customer Success Assistant, AI & Data · Incourage Insurance
The company's chatbot was slow and inconsistent, so customers gave up and called instead. I analysed real conversations to find where it failed, then redesigned its response logic.
Response time dropped by about a third. That's the difference between a customer who stays and one who hangs up.
May 2025 – Present
Research Scholar · iLabAfrica, Strathmore University
Government offices and hospitals across Kenya have software with no easy way to read their own data. I build full production systems end to end, database through dashboard, and design the AI that sits inside them.
I'm the person who takes an AI idea from wish list to something a non-technical office uses every day.
2025 – Present
Collaborator · Vondetta
East African insurers and county governments want AI they can trust, not a black box. I work with a small group of Kenyan engineers under Vondetta, an AI research brand, building underwriting, fraud, and triage tools that are actually explainable. Four are already live.
It's where I get to test ideas outside a single employer's mandate, alongside people who care about the same thing I do.
Where This Is Going

My career is still short on titles and long on finished work. In under three years I went from reading hospital claims for fraud, to pricing insurance applications, to building AI decision systems, to running my own company doing all three at once. Every stop asked a version of the same question: what does this number mean for the person who has to live with the decision it produces? That's the question I bring into any room.
